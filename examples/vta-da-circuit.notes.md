# VTA DA example

Allen native sagittal atlas 2, image 100883900, section 180. Source and verified anchors are in the adjacent audit JSON.

- VTA DA is a population glyph anchored to VTA, not a DA-cell spatial mask.
- NAc uses Allen ACB without core/shell-specific geometry. PL is a component of mPFC.
- Rounded boutons indicate DA projection direction, not a universal activation/inhibition effect. Cerebellar shading and white branches follow source paths.

Evidence: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2011.03.025 ; https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2017.06.009 .
